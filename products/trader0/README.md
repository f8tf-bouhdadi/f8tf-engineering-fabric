# TRADER0 Engineering Service

## TRADER0-ES — RM-ODP Trading Function Engineering Service

### Engineering Promise

Discover and select Engineering Services through governed Service Offers.

### Customer Need

Use TRADER0-ES when Engineering Services or client applications need to
publish, discover and select Service Offers through a governed engineering
registry.

This is relevant when service discovery must remain auditable, repeatable and
independent from the underlying runtime realization.

### Engineering Concern

TRADER0-ES addresses engineering concerns related to:

- Service Offer publication;
- governed service discovery;
- service selection;
- registry queries;
- binding planning;
- engineering evidence.

### Engineering Capability

TRADER0-ES provides the capability to publish, discover, query and select
Engineering Service Offers and to produce Binding Plans for subsequent
realization.

Binding realization is delegated to NET0.

### Service Contract

The service defines explicit operations for:

- publishing Service Offers;
- discovering available Service Offers;
- querying service registries;
- selecting Service Offers;
- producing Binding Plans.

The underlying implementation remains separated from the public service
contract.

### Engineering Guarantees

Current TRADER0-ES guarantees include:

- governed publication;
- deterministic Service Offer resolution;
- idempotent publication;
- immutable published versions;
- engineering evidence generation;
- audit-ready delivery.

Guarantees remain bounded by the declared service scope and current V1
maturity.

### Verification and Evidence

TRADER0-ES is progressively qualified through:

- runnable bounded realization at V0;
- audit-ready specification at V1;
- formal-model preparation at V2_READY;
- formal proving at V2.

Current evidence may include:

- publication logs;
- Service Offer registry entries;
- audit packages;
- RM-ODP specifications;
- UML models.

### Evaluation

A TRADER0 Engineering PoC evaluates governed publication and service discovery
against explicit acceptance criteria.

The evaluation may verify:

- successful Service Offer publication;
- deterministic discovery;
- governed selection;
- idempotent publication;
- reproducible engineering evidence.
