# F8TF Data Engineering Services

This directory exposes Data Engineering Services.

An Engineering Service may be defined independently or may result from a
qualified Engineering Service Composition.

Each Engineering Service defines its own:

- Engineering Promise;
- Customer Need;
- Engineering Concern;
- Engineering Capability;
- Service Contract;
- Engineering Guarantees;
- Verification and Evidence;
- Evaluation path.

Current publicly separated service definitions:

- [DATA0-ES — Foundational Immutable Data Persistence Engineering Service](DATA0/)

A relationship between Engineering Services is not implied by numbering or
catalog order. Relationships are declared through qualified Engineering
Service Compositions.
