# F8TF Data Services

F8TF Data Services provide reusable Interaction Services for recurring
distributed-system concerns involving persistence, deterministic retrieval,
traceability, state management and auditability.

The public portfolio is developed progressively. Each Interaction Service is
given its own explicit service boundary, guarantees, qualification material
and lifecycle products as they are established.

## Available Data Services

### DATA0-ES — Foundational Immutable Data Persistence

**Need**

Record engineering data while preserving the existing data baseline.

**Interaction promise**

Preserve engineering data without destructive mutation.

**Useful for**

Persistence, verification, replay, traceability and audit.

**Availability**

Public Qualified Runtime Delivery and Standard Qualified Delivery.

[Explore DATA0-ES](engineering-services/DATA0/)

### DATA1-ES — Deterministic Audit Query

**Need**

Retrieve engineering evidence through a stable contractual interface with
deterministic and repeatable results.

**Interaction promise**

Retrieve engineering evidence through deterministic and repeatable audit
queries.

**Useful for**

Verification, compliance, traceability and engineering audit.

**Availability**

Standard Qualified Delivery is public. A qualified runtime realization exists
within the declared qualified scope.

[Explore DATA1-ES](engineering-services/DATA1/)

## Data Service Portfolio

The following service identities define the current direction of the broader
F8TF Data Services portfolio.

They are introduced here as portfolio capabilities. Their individual public
service surfaces and lifecycle products are established progressively.

### DATA2-ES — Persistent Document Storage

Manage engineering documents through a contractual persistent storage service.

### DATA3-ES — Versioned Key-Value Index

Manage versioned engineering entries through deterministic, idempotent
operations.

### DATA4-ES — Distributed Data

Manage distributed engineering data through bounded replication and failover.

### DATA5-ES — Bounded Workflow Orchestration

Orchestrate bounded engineering workflows through explicit contracts, ordered
execution, traceability and reproducible verification evidence.

### DATA6-ES — Service Execution Observability

Measure and trace Engineering Service executions through contractual metric
events, append-only recording, bounded service-level evaluation and
reproducible audit evidence.

### DATA7-ES — Interaction Identity Preservation

Preserve coherent logical identity across Engineering Service interactions
through contractual identity contexts, explicit validation, controlled
propagation and reproducible audit evidence.

### DATA8-ES — Bounded Replication Policy Enforcement

Validate and apply a bounded replication policy to a distributed-data service
and produce reproducible evidence of supported write-quorum enforcement.

### DATA9-ES — Bounded Deterministic Shard Routing

Validate a bounded shard-routing policy, deterministically select a logical
shard identifier for each supplied routing key and produce reproducible routing
evidence.

## Progressive Portfolio Development

F8TF develops Data Services incrementally.

A portfolio identity indicates an engineering direction. Availability,
qualification status and lifecycle products are stated on the individual
service surface when that service is publicly established.

This separation allows the portfolio to evolve without implying that every
listed service has the same public lifecycle maturity.

## Engineering and Technical Depth

An established F8TF Interaction Service may expose technical material including:

- its Customer Need and Engineering Promise;
- its explicit Service Contract;
- bounded interaction guarantees;
- RM-ODP engineering specifications;
- qualification and verification material;
- reproducible evidence;
- qualified runtime or delivery products where available.

Technical depth remains attached to the individual Interaction Service rather
than to this family navigation page.
