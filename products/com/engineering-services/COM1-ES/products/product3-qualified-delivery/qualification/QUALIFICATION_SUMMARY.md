# COM1 V1 Service Qualification Summary

The captured COM1 Service Contract V1 qualification evaluates all four declared
COM1 Service Guarantees:

- `G-COM1-BOUNDED`;
- `G-COM1-COMPLETE`;
- `G-COM1-RESPONSE`;
- `G-COM1-ISOLATION`.

The qualification includes:

- baseline COM1 verification;
- execution against the real structured COM1 runtime;
- structured Service Guarantee evaluation;
- Runtime Delivery verification;
- COM1-ES Service Contract qualification at V1.

## Explicit limitations

This V1 qualification does not claim:

- distributed routing;
- request correlation;
- durable audit logging;
- transport confidentiality;
- client authentication;
- high availability;
- production-scale performance;
- formal proof.
