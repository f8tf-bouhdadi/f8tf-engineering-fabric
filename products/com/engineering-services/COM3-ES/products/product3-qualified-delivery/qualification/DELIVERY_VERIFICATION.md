## Verification Boundary

LCS-13 verifies:

- delivery completeness;
- preservation of the Qualified Runtime identity;
- integrity of the runtime components;
- presence of the documented Operator surface;
- presence of the documented configuration model;
- independent operational execution of the delivered runtime.

LCS-13 introduces no new COM3 Service Guarantee.
