# F8TF Customer Model

## Purpose

The F8TF Customer Model describes how organizations progressively discover,
evaluate and adopt F8TF Engineering Capabilities.

It is the reference model governing the public customer experience.

## Customer Journey

Customer Need

↓

Solution

↓

Engineering Capability

↓

Engineering Product

↓

Engineering PoC

↓

Engineering Guarantees

↓

Engineering Evidence

↓

Customer Decision

## Customer Need

Every customer starts with an engineering challenge.

F8TF is organized around customer needs rather than around technologies.

## Solution

A solution explains how F8TF addresses a class of engineering challenges.

Solutions are described using customer language.

## Engineering Capability

Engineering Capabilities describe what organizations become able to achieve.

Capabilities bridge customer needs and Engineering Products.

## Engineering Product

Engineering Products realize one or more Engineering Capabilities.

Products package Engineering Services, contracts, guarantees, verification
and evidence into coherent offerings.

## Engineering PoC

Engineering PoCs allow customers to evaluate bounded capabilities before
adoption.

## Engineering Guarantees

Guarantees define explicit engineering properties together with their scope,
verification and maturity.

## Engineering Evidence

Evidence supports engineering claims through reproducible verification.

## Customer Decision

Every step of the customer journey contributes to an informed adoption
decision.

## Design Principles

- Customer-first.
- Capability-driven.
- Product-enabled.
- Evidence-based.
- Trust through explicit guarantees.
- Publish WHAT — Keep HOW private.
