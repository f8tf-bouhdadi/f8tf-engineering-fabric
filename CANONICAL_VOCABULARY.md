# F8TF Canonical Vocabulary

## Purpose

Define the official terminology used throughout the F8TF public customer interface.

Every public page should use this vocabulary consistently.

## Customer Journey Terms

- Customer Need
- Engineering Concern
- Engineering Discipline
- Engineering Capability
- Engineering Product
- Engineering PoC
- Engineering Guarantee
- Engineering Evidence
- Customer Decision

## Preferred Vocabulary

Use:

- Engineering Capability
- Engineering Product
- Engineering Discipline
- Engineering Guarantee
- Engineering Evidence
- Engineering PoC

Avoid replacing them with generic synonyms.

## Customer Principle

Public pages explain engineering using a consistent language that remains stable
across the entire repository.
