# F8TF Customer Interface

## Mission

The public repository is the official customer interface of F8TF.

Its purpose is to help every visitor understand how F8TF can solve engineering
challenges related to distribution and openness throughout the entire system
lifecycle.

## Design Principles

- Every visitor should find a relevant path within 30 seconds.
- Every page answers a customer question.
- Engineering Products are introduced progressively.
- The repository is organized around customer needs.
- The Engineering Factory remains private.
- Publish WHAT — Keep HOW private.

## Scope

Public repository:

- Customer experience
- Solutions
- Engineering Capabilities
- Products
- Public documentation
- Public guarantees
- Evaluation journey

Private Factory:

- Engineering assets
- Internal automation
- Runtime implementation
- Delivery factories
- Customer workspaces
- Commercial assets
