# Contact F8TF

F8TF develops reusable Engineering Services for recurring distributed-system
interaction and data concerns, with explicit Service Guarantees and qualified
lifecycle products.

## When to Contact F8TF

Contact F8TF if you want to:

- evaluate an F8TF Engineering Service for a concrete system need;
- discuss integration of an Assured Integration-Ready Engineering Service
  Specification;
- evaluate a Qualified Runtime or Qualified Delivery;
- request implementation access where the public service surface indicates that
  access may be provided;
- discuss a proof of concept or engineering evaluation;
- explore platform, integrator, ISV or engineering partnership opportunities.

## Technical Questions and Evaluation Requests

For technical questions, service evaluation requests and public engineering
discussion, use GitHub Discussions:

[F8TF Engineering Questions & Evaluation Requests](https://github.com/f8tf-bouhdadi/f8tf-engineering-fabric/discussions/1)

When possible, identify:

- the Engineering Service you are evaluating;
- the system or interaction concern you want to address;
- the lifecycle product of interest;
- the guarantees or integration questions you want to examine.
