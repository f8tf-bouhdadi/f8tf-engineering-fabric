# F8TF Engineering Capabilities

Engineering Capabilities describe what F8TF enables organizations to achieve
when addressing recurring concerns in open distributed systems.

They connect customer context, F8TF solutions and Engineering Products.

## Evidence and Verification

Capabilities include:

- defining verifiable engineering claims;
- executing bounded verification actions;
- producing traceable evidence;
- supporting audit and acceptance.

## Policy and Governance

Capabilities include:

- defining explicit policies;
- evaluating admissibility;
- governing lifecycle transitions;
- enforcing bounded decision rules.

## Trust, Identity and Security

Capabilities include:

- declaring trust boundaries;
- attributing identity;
- applying authorization constraints;
- preserving security-relevant evidence.

## Communication and Binding

Capabilities include:

- declaring communication channels;
- evaluating binding admissibility;
- applying trust and policy requirements;
- producing traceable binding outcomes.

## Discovery and Selection

Capabilities include:

- publishing service offers;
- discovering and querying services;
- selecting services deterministically;
- producing binding preparation information.

## Data and State Management

Capabilities include:

- persistent recording;
- deterministic retrieval;
- versioning;
- idempotent updates;
- replication and partitioning policies;
- traceable state evolution.

## Observability and Operational Control

Capabilities include:

- exposing system activity;
- recording workflow outcomes;
- surfacing failures;
- supporting operational review.

## Capability Realization

Each capability is realized by one or more Engineering Products and may be
evaluated before adoption.
